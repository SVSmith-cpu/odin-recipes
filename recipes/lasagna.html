Lasagna recipe

Ingredients:

  • 300 g lasagna noodles, uncooked
  • 200 g ground beef
  • 200 g ground Italian pork sausage
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 200 can Italian-seasoned stewed
  • tomatoes
  • 350 g cans tomato paste
  • 2 tbsp Italian seasoning
  • 3 cup ricotta cheese
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 2 tbsp dried parsley
  • salt and pepper to taste
  • 450 g shredded mozzarella chees

Method:

  1. Cook noodles according to package directions; drain.
  2. Meanwhile, in a skillet over medium heat, brown beef, sausage and onion; drain.
  3. Add garlic, undrained tomatoes, tomato paste and Italian seasoning; simmer for about 10 minutes.
  4. In a large bowl, blend together ricotta and Parmesan cheeses, eggs and seasonings.
  5. Spread 1/2 cup of beef mixture in a lightly greased deep baking pan.
  6. Layer as follows: 1/3 each of noodles, cheese mixture, beef mixture and mozzarella cheese.
  7. Repeat layers, ending with mozzarella.
  8. Cover with aluminum foil.
  9. Bake at 175 degrees for 45 minutes to one hour.
  10. Let stand for 10 minutes before slicing

Bon appetit!
